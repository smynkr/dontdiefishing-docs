Alerts are prompts to reopen the app and reassess a saved launch site or trip.
They are not a guarantee that conditions are safe, that a notification will
reach you, or that anyone is monitoring your trip.

<Warning>
  Recheck current conditions, official weather and marine warnings, applicable
  rules and closures, and your vessel limits before departure. An alert is a
  snapshot and does not replace an official source or an on-site assessment.
</Warning>

## Saved-site alert sensitivity

Saved-site alerts compare current and (when available for the account's tier)
forward-day observations against the previous observation for the same saved
site and default vessel. Choose the account-level **ALERT SENSITIVITY** option
in Settings:

| Preference | Changes that can generate an event |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimal** | A direct **GO ↔ NO-GO** transition. |
| **Standard** | Any status transition, including changes to or from **CAUTION**. |
| **All changes** | Any status transition, or a score change of at least 5 points. |

The option changes which observations qualify; it does not turn device
notifications on. Unavailable observations are not treated as **NO-GO** alerts,
and unchanged readings do not create continuous updates.

## Delivery and permissions

The web app uses browser Web Push for saved-site and monitored-trip notifications.
Sign in and allow notifications for DontDieFishing in the browser over HTTPS. If
permission is blocked or an old subscription has expired, correct the browser's
site permission and retry; you can also reopen the site to recheck the current
data instead of relying on an alert.

Expo/native push registration and server delivery code exists, and mobile
Settings has a separate device-notification control. However, the source release
record says there is no public App Store or Google Play build and no completed
real-device delivery verification. Do not rely on native push as a released or
verified service.

Saved-site condition alerts are push events, not email or SMS. The trip
check-in escalation is separate: after a configured return time plus its
two-hour grace period, the monitor can send an owner-facing push and attempt an
email to the shore contact; contact-email delivery is environment-gated. See
[Trip plans and safety](/dontdiefishing/trips-and-safety).

## Monitored trips

Active trip plans are checked by a server monitor scheduled every 30 minutes.
A status transition can create Web Push and native-push deliveries when eligible
subscriptions exist. The monitor can also attempt a pre-trip brief. Delivery
acceptance does not prove that you saw the alert or that conditions remain
unchanged. Open the plan, refresh, and verify current official sources.

See [Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions),
[Trip plans and safety](/dontdiefishing/trips-and-safety), and
[Account & billing](/dontdiefishing/account-billing).
