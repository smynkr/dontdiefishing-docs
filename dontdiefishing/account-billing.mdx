Use **Settings** to manage your profile, subscription, notifications, vessels, analytics consent, and account data.

## Sign in and create an account

On the web, you can:

- Create an account with an email address and password.
- Sign in with an email address and password.
- Continue with Google or Apple.

Email sign-up sends you to the sign-in page with a reminder to check your email and confirm the account. The web app protects the dashboard, trips, sites, settings, and other app pages; an unauthenticated visit is sent to **Sign in** and the requested path is retained for the redirect after authentication.

## Profile settings

The **Account** section in Settings lets you edit your **Display name** and save it. Your current email is shown as a read-only field. The page notes that email changes go through a confirmation link rather than being edited inline.

Saving the display name updates your profile and refreshes the page. If the session is no longer available, Settings asks you to sign in again; a failed profile update leaves an error so you can retry.

## Free and Pro

The Free tier includes the core app experience. The current Pro card lists these Pro perks:

- Everything in Free
- Full 7-day fishable calendar
- Week-ahead comparison across saved sites
- Next-GO day surfaced automatically

The subscription card offers two billing intervals:

| Interval | Price shown in the product |
| --- | --- |
| Yearly | $39.99/yr (shown as $3.33/mo equivalent; save ~33%) |
| Monthly | $4.99/mo |

The Free card labels the offer **CANCEL ANYTIME**. The exact price and availability shown in the product are the source of truth for checkout.

## Start Pro checkout

From Settings, choose **YEARLY** or **MONTHLY**. The web app sends the selected interval to its authenticated checkout endpoint, then redirects you to the returned Stripe Checkout URL. Checkout accepts valid promotion codes configured in Stripe.

An account with an active Pro subscription cannot start a second Pro checkout. Use the billing portal instead. If checkout cannot be prepared, Settings shows an error and keeps you on the account page.

## Renewal and billing portal

For an active Pro subscription, Settings reads the subscription expiry value and shows **RENEWS** with the date when one is available. The entitlement is reconciled from the customer's Stripe subscription state; a customer with no active configured Pro subscription is treated as Free.

Active Pro accounts have two actions:

- **BILLING PORTAL** opens a Stripe billing portal session and returns to Settings.
- **INVOICES** uses the same billing-portal action, where the product exposes billing documents.

If no Stripe subscription is bound to the account, the portal endpoint reports that no subscription was found. If the portal cannot be opened, Settings shows an error so you can retry.

## Delete your account

The Settings **Danger zone** explains the impact before you request deletion:

- Trips are removed.
- Tracks are removed.
- Logbook entries are removed.
- Float plan links stop working immediately.

The **DELETE ACCOUNT** control links to the product's Contact page. Use that contact path to request account deletion; the Settings page does not delete the account inline.
