title: "Safety & conditions"
description: "How DontDieFishing collects conditions, calculates a planning score, and handles warnings and missing data."
---

DontDieFishing combines current condition inputs into a 0–100 planning score for
the launch site and vessel profile you select. The score is a planning aid, not
a safety clearance.

## Data sources

The conditions aggregator fetches the configured sources in parallel. It records
the resulting snapshot with a `fetched_at` timestamp; it does not promise a
single refresh cadence for every source.

| Data used | Source | What the aggregator does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wind speed and gusts | NWS forecast, with NDBC buoy fallback | Uses the forecast value first, then the buoy value when the forecast field is unavailable. |
| Wave height, wave period, and swell direction | NDBC buoy | Adds buoy observations when a buoy is configured and returns data. |
| Visibility, precipitation probability, air temperature, and lightning risk | NWS forecast | Supplies the forecast weather fields used by the snapshot. |
| Marine warnings | NWS marine alerts | Adds the active warning strings to the snapshot. |
| Tide height and phase | NOAA CO-OPS tide predictions | Selects the next predicted tide event for the snapshot. |
| Water temperature | NOAA CO-OPS, with NDBC buoy fallback | Uses the CO-OPS value first, then buoy water temperature. |
| Tidal current speed | NOAA CO-OPS current predictions | Uses the configured current station and bin when available. |
| UV index | EPA UV | Adds the UV index for the launch coordinates. |
| Sunrise and sunset | Sun-times calculation | Computes times from the launch coordinates, date, and resolved timezone. |

The aggregator currently sets `water_quality` to `null`; it is therefore an
unavailable signal rather than a claim about water quality. A buoy, tide
station, or current station that is not configured also produces an unavailable
value.

## Safety status

The scorer clamps the final result to 0–100 and maps it to one of three
statuses:

| Score | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–39 | **GO** | Lower modeled risk from the available inputs. |
| 40–59 | **CAUTION** | Elevated modeled risk; review the contributing signals and official guidance. |
| 60–100 | **NO-GO** | High modeled risk, or a hard veto has stopped the score. |

The score is not permission to launch. Conditions can change after a snapshot
is collected, and a GO result does not replace official weather, marine-warning,
or navigational judgment.

### Vessel-specific thresholds and profiles

The selected vessel type supplies default thresholds. Values are wind and gust
in knots, waves in feet, visibility in miles, current in knots, and water
temperature in °F.

| Vessel type | Wind / gust | Waves | Visibility | Current | Water temperature | Lightning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Powerboat | 25 / 35 | 6 | 1 | 4 | 45 | Moderate |
| Sailboat | 30 / 40 | 8 | 1 | 4 | 45 | Moderate |
| Kayak | 15 / 20 | 2 | 0.5 | 2 | 55 | Low |
| Paddleboard | 15 / 20 | 2 | 0.5 | 2 | 55 | Low |

You can override threshold values for a vessel with custom thresholds. The
selected scoring profile—**cautious**, **moderate**, or **experienced**—changes
the relative weights of the signals. The [canonical scoring
guide](/dontdiefishing/how-scoring-works) lists those weights and the
normalization rules.

### Hard vetoes

Veto checks run before weighted scoring. Any of these conditions returns a
hard NO-GO:

- An active marine warning contains **small craft advisory**, **hurricane**,
  **tropical storm**, **gale warning**, or **storm warning** (including the
  hurricane watch/warning phrases).
- Lightning risk is **high**.
- Wind speed, gusts, waves, or tidal current are more than 1.5 times the
  selected vessel threshold.
- Visibility is less than half the selected visibility threshold.
- Water temperature is below 75% of the selected water-temperature threshold.

A veto returns a score of `100`, status **NO-GO**, `vetoed: true`, and a reason.
The veto is returned before signal rows are built, so a vetoed result has no
weighted signal breakdown.

### Signal breakdown

When no hard veto fires, the score can show these signal rows:

- Wind / gusts
- Waves / swell
- Marine warnings
- Visibility
- Tidal current
- Lightning
- Precipitation
- Water quality
- UV index
- Water temperature
- Optional travel time

The scorer reports each row's raw value, normalized risk, adjusted weight,
contribution, and whether the value is available. Tide height and phase,
sunrise/sunset, air temperature, wave period, and swell direction can be
presented in the conditions view but are not weighted signal rows in the shared
scorer.

### Marine-warning callout

Any active string warning is shown in the NWS marine-warning callout. The
callout says that the warning was reported in the displayed conditions update
and may have changed. A marine warning does **not** automatically veto every
score: only the warning phrases listed in the hard-veto rules do. Check current
official NWS guidance before departure.

## Freshness and unknown values

The web conditions endpoint reuses a cached snapshot only when it is less than
30 minutes old. When no fresh snapshot exists, it calls the aggregator; if that
fetch fails, the endpoint returns an error instead of returning a score from an
unknown fetch.

At signal level, missing values are marked unavailable. Their base weight is
excluded and redistributed across available signals; the missing value is not a
measured favorable condition. The shared scorer does not infer a per-signal
freshness state (`is_stale` is `false` in its returned rows), so treat a missing
or unverified source as unknown. Read unknown as fail-closed for trip planning:
do not interpret it as GO, and verify the official source before departure.
