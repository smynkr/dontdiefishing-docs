---
title: "How scoring works"
description: "The DontDieFishing planning score: available inputs, vessel thresholds, signal normalization, weights, freshness, and hard vetoes."
---

The shared scorer converts available launch-site conditions into a 0–100
planning score for the selected vessel. Lower is lower modeled risk. The order
is: choose default and custom vessel thresholds, choose the profile and custom
weights, check hard vetoes, build the available signal rows, renormalize their
weights, then round, clamp, and classify the result.

<Warning>
  This is a planning model, not a probability, forecast guarantee, navigation
  instrument, or authorization to launch. Forecasts are uncertain; a stale score,
  missing signal, or incomplete source set is not evidence of safe conditions.
  Verify current official guidance and conditions before departure.
</Warning>

## Status bands

| Final score | Status | How to read it |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 0–39 | **GO** | Lower modeled risk from the scored inputs that were available. |
| 40–59 | **CAUTION** | Elevated modeled risk; inspect the contributing signals. |
| 60–100 | **NO-GO** | High modeled risk. A hard veto also returns 100 / NO-GO. |

## Signals

The web aggregator uses the following inputs when available:

| Signal | Source and scoring behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Wind / gusts | NWS forecast, with NDBC buoy fallback for missing values; the higher normalized wind/gust risk is used. |
| Waves / swell | NDBC buoy first, with NWS grid waves as fallback; wave height is scored. Period and swell direction are display details. |
| Marine warnings | Active NWS alert text is shown; the scorer normalizes recognized warning language and some phrases veto. |
| Visibility | NWS forecast; unavailable where the forecast does not provide a usable value. |
| Tidal current | NOAA CO-OPS current prediction, only where a station and bin are configured. |
| Lightning | NWS forecast text mapped to none, low, moderate, or high. High is a hard veto. |
| Precipitation | NWS forecast probability, clamped to 0–100. |
| Water temperature | NOAA CO-OPS first, then NDBC buoy where configured. |
| UV index | EPA UV data. |
| Water quality | Present in the shared model, but the current aggregator supplies `null`; it is unavailable. |
| Travel time | Optional scorer input; scales from 0 at 0 minutes to 100 at 120 minutes. |

Tide height/phase, sunrise/sunset, air temperature, wave period, and swell
direction can be shown in the conditions view but are not weighted score rows.
The current aggregator does not use USGS streamflow.

## Vessel thresholds

Default thresholds are adjusted by the selected vessel type; custom thresholds
can override them.

| Vessel type | Wind (kts) | Gust (kts) | Waves (ft) | Visibility (mi) | Current (kts) | Water temperature (°F) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Powerboat | 25 | 35 | 6 | 1 | 4 | 45 |
| Sailboat | 30 | 40 | 8 | 1 | 4 | 45 |
| Kayak | 15 | 20 | 2 | 0.5 | 2 | 55 |
| Paddleboard | 15 | 20 | 2 | 0.5 | 2 | 55 |

There is no vessel-specific lightning threshold. High lightning risk is a hard
veto for every vessel.

## Normalization and weights

Wind, gusts, wave height, and tidal current use a piecewise scale: 0 at the
ideal value, 70 at the vessel threshold, and 100 at 1.5 times that threshold.
Visibility and water temperature use inverse scales: visibility is 0 at 10
miles, 70 at its threshold, and 100 at half the threshold; water temperature is
0 at 70°F, 70 at its threshold, and 100 at half the threshold.

Lightning maps to none = 0, low = 30, moderate = 70, high = 100. Precipitation
is its percentage. UV rises from 0 at index 0 to 70 at index 8 and 100 at 12.
Marine warning text maps advisory = 40, watch = 70, warning = 100; the highest
recognized warning is used, and an unrecognized non-empty warning defaults to
40. Water-quality values have model mappings but are unavailable from the
current aggregator.

The three profiles use these base weights before missing signals are removed:

| Signal | Cautious | Moderate | Experienced |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Wind / gusts | 22% | 20% | 15% |
| Waves / swell | 20% | 18% | 14% |
| Marine warnings | 18% | 15% | 12% |
| Visibility | 12% | 12% | 12% |
| Tidal current | 8% | 10% | 12% |
| Lightning | 8% | 8% | 8% |
| Precipitation | 4% | 5% | 7% |
| Water quality | 3% | 4% | 5% |
| UV index | 2% | 3% | 5% |
| Water temperature | 2% | 3% | 5% |
| Travel time | 1% | 2% | 5% |

Custom weights override profile defaults. Missing numeric signals do not
contribute; the remaining available weights are redistributed proportionally.
Therefore the same physical conditions can produce a different score for a
different vessel, profile, or available signal set.

## Hard vetoes

Veto checks run before weighted scoring. A matching veto returns `100`,
**NO-GO**, `vetoed: true`, a reason, and no weighted signal breakdown:

- A marine warning contains **small craft advisory**, **hurricane**,
  **tropical storm**, **gale warning**, **storm warning**, **hurricane warning**,
  or **hurricane watch**.
- Lightning risk is **high**.
- Wind, gusts, waves, or tidal current exceed 1.5 times the selected threshold.
- Visibility is below half its selected threshold.
- Water temperature is below 75% of its selected threshold.

A warning in the UI is not automatically a veto: only the listed phrases do so.
Check current official NWS guidance. Fishing regulation closures are separate
from these score vetoes and must be verified with the relevant authority.

## Freshness, forecast confidence, and missing data

The web verdict labels snapshot age **FRESH** through 15 minutes, **AGING** from
more than 15 through 30 minutes, and **STALE** after 30 minutes. Missing,
invalid, or future timestamps are **NO DATA**. The authenticated conditions
endpoint uses only an under-30-minute cache; otherwise it fetches new data and
returns an error if the aggregate fetch fails. The site detail marks a displayed
last-known result as stale and offers a retry when an update fails.

The separate **HIGH / MED / LOW** label measures the fraction of score signals
available, not the probability that a forecast or verdict is correct. A recent
`fetched_at` timestamp does not guarantee every provider succeeded. Some source
failures can leave warnings or forecast fields unknown.

Forward Fishable Days results use NWS forecast windows, not live marine
measurements. If a day lacks usable wind data or complete hourly forecast
coverage, the score is unavailable rather than a best-case GO. Future-day
scores omit active marine warnings, waves, tide/current, water temperature, and
UV. See [Fishable Days](/dontdiefishing/fishable-days) and [Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions).
