Common questions and fixes for DontDieFishing.

## General

### Is DontDieFishing free?

Core features — spot browsing, conditions, and hazard warnings — are free.
Premium adds offline maps, extended forecasts, and unlimited saved spots.

### Where does the data come from?

See [Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions) for the full data-source table.
Primary sources are USGS, NOAA, and NWS — all U.S. government services.

### Is my location shared with other users?

No. Your location is used only to center the map and calculate distances.
Other users never see your position.

## Spots

### A spot near me is missing

The spot database is growing. Tap **Suggest a Spot** on the map (long-press
any location) to submit a new fishing spot. Include the name, water type, and
access notes.

### The conditions look wrong

Condition data refreshes on a schedule (see
[Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions)). If data seems stale:

- Check the "Last updated" timestamp on the spot page
- USGS gauges occasionally go offline for maintenance
- Submit a community report with your on-site observation

### Can I use this for saltwater fishing?

Yes. Surf, pier, jetty, and inshore spots include tide data from NOAA CO-OPS
and saltwater species profiles.

## Alerts

### What notifications does DontDieFishing send?

There are two types of push notifications:

- **Status-transition alerts** — when a saved spot's safety status changes
  (e.g., green → yellow), you get an alert if you've enabled alerts for that
  spot. These only fire on a status change, not continuously.
- **Pre-trip brief** — when you have a trip planned for today or tomorrow,
  you receive exactly one brief summarizing the forecast (safety score, wind,
  waves) and which species are currently open. The brief fires automatically
  within the 24-hour window before your planned departure — one per trip, no
  duplicates even if conditions change.

### I am not getting push notifications

- Ensure notifications are enabled for the app in your phone's Settings
- Check that the saved spot has alerts toggled on (spot page → bell icon)
- Status-transition alerts only fire when a spot's status changes (e.g.,
  green → yellow), not on every update
- Pre-trip briefs are sent once per trip, within 24 hours of the planned
  date — if your trip is further out, the brief hasn't triggered yet
