Common questions and fixes for DontDieFishing.

## General

### Is DontDieFishing free?

Core features — spot browsing, conditions, hazard warnings, trip planning,
the logbook (including auto-suggested drafts and multi-species catches), and
GPS track recording and replay — are free. Pro adds photo uploads to
logbook entries, logbook insights (species-by-month trends, per-site catch
counts, and conditions-you-catch-best-in analysis), offline maps, extended
forecasts, and unlimited saved spots.

### Where does the data come from?

See [Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions) for the full data-source table.
Primary sources are USGS, NOAA, and NWS — all U.S. government services.

### Is my location shared with other users?

No. Your location is used only to center the map and calculate distances.
Other users never see your position. GPS track recordings are private to
your account.

## Spots

### A spot near me is missing

The spot database is growing. Tap **Suggest a Spot** on the map (long-press
any location) to submit a new fishing spot. Include the name, water type, and
access notes.

### The conditions look wrong

Condition data refreshes on a schedule (see
[Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions)). If data seems stale:

- Check the "Last updated" timestamp on the spot page
- USGS gauges occasionally go offline for maintenance
- Submit a community report with your on-site observation

### Can I use this for saltwater fishing?

Yes. Surf, pier, jetty, and inshore spots include tide data from NOAA CO-OPS
and saltwater species profiles.

## Logbook

### What is a logbook draft?

When a planned trip's date passes, DontDieFishing automatically creates a
draft logbook entry with the trip's launch site and condition snapshot
already attached. Add your catch details and confirm the entry, or dismiss
it if the trip didn't happen. One draft is created per trip; dismissing it
prevents re-creation.

### Can I log multiple species in one entry?

Yes. Each logbook entry supports multiple catch lines — add species, count,
size, and weight for each. You can also log a skunked trip by submitting an
entry with no catch lines.

### How do photos work?

Photos can be attached to logbook entries (up to 10 per entry, 10 MB each).
Photo upload is a Pro feature. Photos are stored privately in your account
and are not visible to other users.

### What are logbook insights?

Insights are Pro-only aggregates of your confirmed logbook entries:
species-by-month trends, per-site catch counts, and the conditions (wind and
wave buckets) you catch best in. Insights update automatically as you confirm
entries.

## Track recording

### How do I record a GPS track?

From the mobile app's Trips screen, tap **Record** on a monitoring trip (or
use the ad-hoc **Record a track** banner). The app uses your phone's GPS to
capture your boat path. Grant location permissions when prompted; background
recording requires the "always" location permission.

### Where do I replay a track?

Recorded tracks appear on the web app under **Recent tracks** (linked from
the Trips page and individual trip cards). Open a track to see the replay map
with a time-scrubber and speed controls (1×, 2×, 4×).

### How long are tracks stored?

Tracks are retained for 30 days. Tracks linked to a confirmed logbook entry
are kept as long as the entry exists. You can also delete a track manually
from the web app.

## Alerts

### I am not getting push notifications

- Ensure notifications are enabled for the app in your phone's Settings
- Check that the saved spot has alerts toggled on (spot page → bell icon)
- Alerts only fire on status transitions (e.g., green → yellow), not
  continuously
