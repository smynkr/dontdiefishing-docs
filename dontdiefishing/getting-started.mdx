Download DontDieFishing, set your location, and find your first safe fishing
spot.

## Install

Download DontDieFishing from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).

## Set your location

1. Open the app and allow location access (or enter your city manually).
2. The map centers on your area with nearby fishing spots pinned.

## Browse spots

Each pin on the map is a fishing spot. Pins are color-coded by safety status:

- **Green** — conditions are favorable, no active hazards
- **Yellow** — fishable, but check conditions (rising water, wind, etc.)
- **Red** — hazardous right now (flooding, strong currents, closures)

Tap a pin to see the spot detail card: species present, water type (river,
lake, pier, surf), current conditions, and any active hazard warnings.

## Save spots

Tap the bookmark icon on any spot to save it. Saved spots appear in your
**My Spots** tab with condition updates and optional push alerts.

## Check before you go

Before heading out, open the spot detail and review:

1. **Water conditions** — level, flow rate, clarity, temperature
2. **Weather** — wind speed/direction, precipitation, lightning risk
3. **Hazards** — active warnings for the area
4. **Access** — road conditions, parking, walk-in distance

The app aggregates data from USGS stream gauges, NOAA weather, and
community-reported hazards. See [Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions) for
data sources and limitations.
