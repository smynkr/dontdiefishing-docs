title: "Getting started"
description: "Create an account, configure your vessel and safety profile, save a launch site, and plan a trip you can verify before launch."

DontDieFishing is built around launch sites and boat planning. Follow this
sequence: create an account, configure the vessel profile that sets your safety
thresholds, save a launch site, evaluate its risk score, plan a trip, and
verify the latest conditions before launch.

<Warning>
  A score is decision support, not a guarantee of safety. Conditions can change
  faster than the app refreshes, and community reports are observations rather
  than official forecasts. Assess conditions in person before entering the
  water.
</Warning>

## 1. Create your account

Open DontDieFishing and choose **Sign up**. You can use an email and password,
or continue with Google or Apple. Sign in to an existing account when you
return.

After authentication, complete onboarding. The final step saves your vessel
type and scoring profile to your account and takes you to the Dashboard.

## 2. Configure your vessel and safety profile

Onboarding asks two questions:

- **Vessel type** — choose **Power**, **Sail**, **Kayak**, or **Paddle**. This
  creates the starting thresholds used to evaluate conditions.
- **Experience** — choose **Cautious**, **Moderate**, or **Experienced**. This
  controls how aggressively conditions are scored; you can fine-tune the
  thresholds later.

To add or refine a profile after onboarding, open **Settings** and add a
**Vessel Profile**. Give it a name, choose its type, optionally enter its
length, and select a scoring profile. Mark one vessel as the default when you
keep more than one profile.

## 3. Save a launch site

1. Open **Launch Sites**.
2. Choose a region and browse the available launch sites.
3. Select the bookmark control on a site to save it to your account.

Saved sites appear on **Dashboard**, where the app loads their current
conditions. If you have not saved a site yet, Dashboard shows a curated list
until you add one.

## 4. Evaluate the risk score

Use Dashboard to compare your saved sites. Each site card can show a composite
risk score, a status, current conditions, and an hour-by-hour risk proxy. The
status uses three outcomes:

- **GO** — the current score is inside the selected vessel's thresholds.
- **CAUTION** — conditions are approaching those thresholds.
- **NO-GO** — conditions are outside the thresholds.

Lower scores represent lower risk in the planning model. Open a site to inspect
the signal breakdown and the measurements behind the score. If the score is
missing, check that a vessel profile exists and retry the conditions request.

## 5. Plan the trip

Open **Trip Plans** and choose **Plan a Trip**. Select:

- a saved launch site,
- the vessel profile for this trip,
- a date, and
- an optional departure time.

The trip appears in your trip list and in the Dashboard's next-trip panel.
Dashboard compares the planned trip with the vessel limits and keeps the trip
available for a fresh check as the departure approaches.

## 6. Verify before launch

Before leaving, open the planned trip and re-check the current risk score,
conditions, and the change against your vessel limits. Review the site detail
for the available signal breakdown, community observations, and fishing
regulations. Forecast-based results are not a substitute for a final
in-person check or for the applicable official rules.

## App navigation

The web app sidebar uses these product labels:

| Label | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Review saved-site conditions and the next trip. |
| **Launch Sites** | Browse and save launch sites. |
| **Trip Plans** | Create and monitor planned trips. |
| **Fishable Days** **PRO** | Compare the forecast calendar for your vessel. |
| **Regulations** | Review fishing-regulation references. |
| **Logbook** | Record trip notes and catches. |
| **Settings** | Manage your account, vessel profiles, and alerts. |
