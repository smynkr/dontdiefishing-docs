DontDieFishing helps boaters and anglers plan from curated launch sites. Browse
sites by region, save the places you use, review current conditions against a
vessel profile, and keep a trip plan ready for another check before departure.

<Callout type="warn">
  A **GO** score is planning guidance, never a safety clearance or guarantee.
  Forecasts are probabilistic and conditions can change between checks. A
  missing or stale input is not evidence of safety. Check current official
  weather and marine warnings, applicable closures and fishing rules, and the
  conditions at the launch site before you go.
</Callout>

## What the planner covers

- **Launch Sites** — browse the curated catalog by region, save sites, and open
  site detail. The web map is region-based; it does not use your device's
  location. The optional mobile map is a marker-only site view, not a marine
  chart or navigation aid.
- **Conditions** — where sources are available, the current view combines NWS
  weather and warnings, NOAA buoy and tide data, and EPA UV data. A vessel
  profile affects thresholds and score weights. Freshness labels and the
  available-signal count describe the snapshot; confidence is not a probability.
- **Fishable Days** — compare the current day and a limited forward forecast
  window. Free returns one day; Pro returns up to seven. Future-day scores omit
  marine warnings, waves, tides, currents, and other unforecast signals.
- **Trip planning** — create a launch-site and vessel-specific plan, recheck its
  readiness, and optionally share a float plan or configure a return check-in.
- **Fishing-rule references** — selected saltwater listings show a season status
  and citations when current source evidence is available. They are not a
  complete rulebook and do not grant permission to fish or keep a catch.

The source repository also contains a mobile app. Its current code includes a
feature-gated site map and GPS track recording, but the repository's release
record says no public App Store or Google Play build has reached users. See
[Mobile app](/dontdiefishing/mobile-app) for that release boundary and the
implemented mobile flows.
