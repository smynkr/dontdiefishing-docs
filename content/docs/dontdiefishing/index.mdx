DontDieFishing is a boating and fishing safety app that finds you safe spots
with real-time water conditions, weather, and hazard data. Every spot on the
map is color-coded by current safety status, and saved spots can push you an
alert the moment conditions turn.

<Callout type="warn">
  DontDieFishing is a decision-support tool, not a guarantee of safety.
  Conditions can change faster than data refreshes, and community reports are
  unverified observations. Always assess conditions in person before entering
  the water.
</Callout>
