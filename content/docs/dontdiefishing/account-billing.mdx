Use **Settings** in the authenticated web app to manage your account, vessel
profiles, alert preferences, and subscription. The available sign-in providers
and the terms shown at checkout are the source of truth for your session and
purchase.

## Sign in and profile

Create an account with email and password or sign in with a provider offered on
the page. Google sign-in is available; Apple is shown only when the provider is
configured as available. Email confirmation may be required after registration.
Protected app pages return unauthenticated visitors to Sign in and preserve the
requested path for after authentication.

Settings shows your email as read-only and lets you edit or clear your display
name. A failed save displays an error; sign in again if the session has expired.

## Vessel profiles and records

A vessel profile identifies the craft used for scoring and trip planning. Choose
**Power**, **Sail**, **Kayak**, or **Paddle**, set its **Cautious**,
**Moderate**, or **Experienced** scoring profile, and optionally set its length
and custom thresholds or weights. You can add, edit, set a default, or remove a
profile; change the default before deleting the current default vessel.

The web vessel collection also includes personal vessel records: one vessel
photo (up to 10 MB), notes, maintenance entries (service, repair, inspection,
upgrade, or other), and uploaded documents up to 20 MB in supported PDF, Office,
and image formats. These are vessel records, not a shared fishing-tackle or
recommended-gear catalog.

## Free and Pro

Free includes core account, launch-site, condition, and trip-planning screens.
Pro adds the full seven-day Fishable Days response, week-ahead comparisons across
resolved sites, and the next-GO-day summary. The source currently lists these
billing options:

| Interval | Displayed price                                    |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Monthly  | $4.99/mo                                           |
| Yearly   | $39.99/yr ($3.33/mo equivalent; about 33% savings) |

Prices, taxes, promotions, and availability can change; the checkout page is the
current offer. Mobile source currently has no in-app subscription purchase.

## Checkout, renewal, and cancellation

From web Settings, choose the monthly or yearly option to start Stripe Checkout.
An active Pro account cannot start another Pro checkout; use **Billing Portal**
to manage billing. The portal may expose invoices and the cancellation or
renewal controls available for your subscription. If the portal cannot be
opened, Settings shows an error so you can retry. A current checkout display is
not a promise of future price or availability.

## Request account deletion

Settings explains that deleting an account removes trips, tracks, and logbook
entries and ends float-plan links. The **DELETE ACCOUNT** action opens the
product Contact path; Settings does not delete the account inline. Use that
contact path to request deletion.
