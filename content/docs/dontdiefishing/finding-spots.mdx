How to search, filter, and evaluate fishing spots in DontDieFishing.

## Search and filters

Use the search bar or filter panel to narrow spots:

- **Species** — bass, trout, catfish, salmon, panfish, surf species, etc.
- **Water type** — river, lake, pond, pier, jetty, surf, creek
- **Distance** — radius from your location (1–200 miles)
- **Safety status** — show only green (safe) spots, or include yellow
- **Access** — boat launch, shore access, wheelchair-accessible, walk-in only

## Spot detail

Every spot page shows:

### Conditions panel

- Water level and trend (rising / falling / stable)
- Flow rate (cfs) for rivers and streams
- Water temperature
- Visibility / clarity
- Last updated timestamp

### Weather panel

- Current conditions and 24-hour forecast
- Wind speed and direction
- Lightning risk index
- Precipitation probability

### Hazard warnings

- Active warnings (flood stage, strong current, debris, algae bloom)
- Seasonal closures (spawning season, dam operations)
- Community-reported hazards with timestamps

### Regulations

- Size limits, bag limits, and seasonal closures for relevant species
- Sourced from the official state fish and wildlife agency
- Direct link to the official agency page for each rule
- Last-checked date shown on each regulation card

### Species and access

- Fish species commonly caught at this spot
- Best seasons and times of day
- Access notes: parking, walk-in distance, boat launch, fees

## Community reports

Signed-in users can submit condition reports:

- Water clarity and level observations
- Fish activity (biting, surface activity)
- Hazard sightings (debris, erosion, dangerous access)
- Access issues (locked gates, construction, flooding)

Reports are timestamped and attributed. Community reports supplement — but do
not replace — official data sources.
