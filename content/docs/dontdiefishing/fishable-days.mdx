**Fishable Days** is the web app's forecast calendar for the launch sites
returned by the API. It scores each returned day against a selected vessel
profile and uses the same **GO**, **CAUTION**, and **NO-GO** statuses as live
conditions.

<Callout type="warn">
  Forecast scores are planning guidance. Forward days use wind and weather
  forecasts only; they are not a substitute for a final conditions check before
  launching.
</Callout>

## Before you open the calendar

Fishable Days requires a vessel profile. If your account has no vessel, the page
shows **NO VESSEL PROFILE** and links to **Settings**. Add a profile before
trying to score forecast days.

The calendar uses the active vessel from your vessel settings. If no active
vessel is selected, it falls back to the default vessel and then the first
available profile. The header identifies the selected profile with
**SCORED FOR YOUR VESSEL**.

## Saved-site fallback and cap

Fishable Days normally scores your saved launch sites. If the saved-links read
fails or the saved set is empty, the API resolves its curated launch sites
instead. In that fallback, rows are labeled **CURATED**, rather than as saved
sites. The route caps the resolved site list at **12 sites** per response.

## Month and week views

Use the **MONTH** and **WEEK** controls to change the calendar view. The arrows
move the visible month or seven-day range, and selecting a day updates the
summary panel.

### Month view

Month view gives a daily overview. Each day can show:

- the best available score across the returned launch sites,
- its GO, CAUTION, or NO-GO color,
- a compact wind and rain readout, and
- a marker when you already have a trip planned for that day.

Select a day to see the selected date's best score, status, and the first
available **best window** in the launch site's local time zone. The detail panel
also lists every resolved site returned for that date, sorted from the lowest
score to the highest score, so you can compare the launch-site candidates behind
the daily best.

### Week view

Week view is the site-by-site comparison. Rows are the resolved launch sites and
columns are the seven days in the visible range. Each cell can show that site's
score, a wind/rain readout, and a **TRIP** tag when the planned trip uses that
site on that date. Click a cell to inspect the selected day's detail.

## Scores, statuses, and best windows

The calendar uses a lower-is-better risk model. Each launch site/day can have a
score, status, source, and dominant signal. When conditions are available, the
detail panel explains the dominant signal and its raw value. A hard vessel
limit can veto every available window; the panel shows the veto reason when one
is present.

For forecast days, the page labels the result as forecast conditions and calls
out that waves, water temperature, and tides are not modeled in that forward
result. A missing score is shown as unavailable rather than as a favorable
status.

The best-window readout uses the first available solunar window for the selected
site and formats it in that site's time zone. If no window data is available,
the panel says **NO WINDOW DATA**.

## 7-day scoring and the 30-day summary

The calendar has two related surfaces:

- **Site-by-site 7-day scoring (Pro):** Pro returns seven days. Week view
  compares every returned day for every resolved launch site, and month view
  shows the best site for each returned day.
- **NEXT 30 DAYS summary:** the side panel groups the returned days by GO,
  CAUTION, and NO-GO and counts only the days in the current response.

The current product returns **one day on Free** and **seven days on Pro**. The
**NEXT 30 DAYS** heading is a product label for this summary surface; it does
not mean that the API returns 30 site-scored forecast days.

When a Free response is limited to today, the page shows an upgrade prompt for
the seven-day forecast window. The prompt explains that Pro scores the next
seven days against the selected vessel and uses the same GO, CAUTION, and
NO-GO verdicts as live conditions.

## Trip tags and planning

Fishable Days loads your trip plans as optional context. A trip marker in Month
view or a **TRIP** badge in Week view means that you already scheduled a plan
for that date; it does not change the score. Select a scored day and choose
**PLAN THIS DAY** to open the trip form.

## Free and Pro access

Free and Pro share the same vessel-specific scoring model. The difference is
the forecast window:

| Plan     | Forecast access                                                        |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Free** | One returned day (today).                                              |
| **Pro**  | Seven returned days, with week-ahead comparison across resolved sites. |

The Pro upgrade card offers yearly and monthly billing choices. Existing Pro
users see billing-portal and invoice controls instead of an upgrade action.
