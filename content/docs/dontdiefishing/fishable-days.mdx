**Fishable Days** scores a limited set of dates for a selected vessel and launch
site. The calendar uses the same lower-is-better GO / CAUTION / NO-GO scale as
current conditions, but forward days are NWS forecast-based planning results,
not promises of safe conditions.

<Callout type="warn">
  Forecasts are probabilistic and may have gaps. Future-day results do not model
  all live marine conditions or active warnings. Always recheck current weather,
  official marine warnings, closures, regulations, and on-site conditions before
  departure.
</Callout>

## Access and vessel selection

Fishable Days is part of the authenticated web app and requires a vessel profile.
If there is no vessel, the page prompts you to add one in **Settings**. The
selected vessel supplies the thresholds and score profile; change it in the
vessel selector before comparing a different craft.

The current response window is **one day on Free** and **seven days on Pro**.
The **NEXT 30 DAYS** label on the summary is not a promise of 30 scored days; it
summarizes only the days in the current response. The free view can offer an
upgrade prompt for the longer forecast window.

The endpoint normally uses saved launch sites. If the saved-site read fails or
there are no saved sites, it falls back to curated sites; it labels those rows
**CURATED**. No more than 12 sites are returned at a time.

## Month and week views

Use **MONTH** or **WEEK** to change the visible calendar. The arrows move the
month or seven-day range, and selecting a day opens its summary.

- **Month:** each returned day shows the best available score among resolved
  sites, a status/color, a compact wind/rain summary, and a trip marker if you
  already planned a trip that day. The day detail shows the first available
  best window in the site's local time and compares available sites from lower
  score to higher score.
- **Week:** rows compare sites and columns show days. A **TRIP** badge means a
  trip plan uses that site on that date; the plan marker does not change the
  score.

A planned trip is context, not an input to the forecast score. Choose a scored
day and **PLAN THIS DAY** to open the trip form.

## Live day and forecast days

The current day can use a live conditions snapshot. Forward days are built from
NWS forecast data and site-local calendar dates. They score available wind and
gusts, precipitation, and forecast-derived visibility/lightning signals; marine
warnings are active-only and are not forecast. Waves, tide height/phase,
tidal current, water temperature, UV, and water quality are not included in
forward-day scores. A **FORECAST** label does not mean that each source is
present or that the result is a guarantee.

A daily summary with usable wind can supply a forecast window. Otherwise the
system requires complete hourly coverage and usable wind signals for the local
day. If weather periods are missing, invalid, or incomplete, that date stays
unscored. It is shown as unavailable, not assigned a favorable status from the
few hours that happened to arrive. A hard vessel limit can still veto a scored
window to **NO-GO**.

The best-window detail uses the first available solunar window for the selected
site and formats it in that site's timezone. Solunar windows are optional
planning context, not a safety or catch guarantee; if unavailable, the page
says **NO WINDOW DATA**.

## Safety and official sources

A forecast score is not the same as the latest conditions score. A source age or
confidence label is not the probability that a forecast is right. Read the
signal and source information, then verify the official forecast and active
warnings directly. Regulatory closure status is a separate reference view; it
is not incorporated as a guarantee that the site or trip is legal.

See [How scoring works](/dontdiefishing/how-scoring-works) for status bands and
vessel thresholds, [Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions) for
freshness and source limitations, and [Trip plans and safety](/dontdiefishing/trips-and-safety)
for planned-trip readiness.
