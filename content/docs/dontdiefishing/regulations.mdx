The public **Regulations** catalog is a selected reference for saltwater
recreational fishing rules in supported jurisdictions. It normalizes cited
source rows so you can review listed season windows, statuses, and limits. It is
not a complete rulebook for every state, freshwater fishery, location, method,
or emergency closure.

<Callout type="warn">
  A catalog label is not permission to fish or retain a catch. Rules can vary by
  location, method, vessel, species identification, and emergency order. Check
  the linked authority's current rule immediately before fishing or keeping a
  catch.
</Callout>

## Browse the catalog

Choose a jurisdiction, then search its listed species. The web catalog can
search species name, slug, applicability notes, authority, and possession-limit
text. Status controls filter **OPEN NOW**, **CLOSING SOON**, **CLOSED**,
**YEAR-ROUND**, or all listed entries. Site detail may show a smaller set of
jurisdiction-specific regulation cards and an **Official rules** link.

State pages also expose curated launch-site cards when available; **ALL SITES**
opens the launch-site directory. A species page may include its profile,
regulation references, and sourced field-guide content. The catalog does not
provide a fishing-tackle or gear-recommendation feature.

## What a regulation listing means

A normalized record can include season windows, possession-limit text, bag
limit, minimum or maximum size, applicability notes, authority, citation, source
URL, source update/check dates, and effective year. The catalog uses the
jurisdiction and launch site's local timezone to evaluate the current season.
The displayed limit and size fields refer to the cited rule, not an estimate of
your catch.

| Status           | Meaning                                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **OPEN**         | A current, provenance-backed rule has an active year-round or scheduled window.                                        |
| **CLOSING SOON** | An active scheduled window closes within seven calendar days.                                                          |
| **CLOSED**       | The cited rule explicitly closes the species, no scheduled window is active, or the active window's bag limit is zero. |
| **VERIFY**       | Current status cannot be established safely because evidence is missing, stale, mismatched, malformed, or ambiguous.   |

**VERIFY is not the same as CLOSED, and neither status proves that a separate
emergency closure does not exist.** When current status cannot be verified, the
catalog withholds current-looking limits; a page may retain a clearly labeled
last-listed reference value.

Rules are evaluated against the effective year and site-local calendar. An
incomplete citation, invalid timezone, stale source check, wrong year, invalid
schedule, or overlapping season windows fails closed to **VERIFY**. A source
check older than seven days cannot authorize a current badge. A PDF or state web
page can itself be superseded; use the official source link for current rules,
emergency actions, and local qualifications.

Some agency and CDN source pages can block automated checks. In that case the
app fails closed rather than inventing a source check or freshness date. A
**VERIFY** result means you must use the linked authority directly.

## Species field guides are not fishing rules

Species pages can show a one-line description, habitat, scientific name, and a
sourced typical-size note. A smaller set of guides has passed a deeper content
review; those entries may also show identification, field marks, lookalikes,
habitat/depth, seasonality, techniques, handling/release, conservation context,
and regulation pointers with citations. Candidate or draft deep sections are
withheld instead of being presented as verified. Mobile's guide API likewise
returns verified entries only.

Species guide size is not a record-size promise, and a field guide is not a
substitute for a current agency rule or an authoritative identification. Follow
its citations. The app has no fishing-tackle or recommended-gear catalog.

## Closures and safety are separate

The fishing-regulation catalog is separate from the conditions score and from
NWS marine warnings. A regulation closure does not automatically become a
GO/NO-GO score veto, and a GO score does not establish legal access. Check
current agency orders, local access rules, and safety warnings independently.

See [Finding spots](/dontdiefishing/finding-spots) for launch-site details and
[Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions) for score limitations.
