Common questions and fixes for DontDieFishing.

## General

### Is DontDieFishing free?

Core features — spot browsing, conditions, and hazard warnings — are free.
The current Pro card lists:

- A full 7-day fishable calendar
- Week-ahead comparison across saved sites
- The next-GO day surfaced automatically

The current Pro card does **not** list offline maps, extended forecasts, or
unlimited saved spots as Pro perks.

### Where does the data come from?

See [Safety & conditions](/dontdiefishing/safety-conditions) for the full
data-source table. Primary sources include USGS, NOAA, and NWS services.

### Is my location shared with other users?

When GPS track recording is enabled, the mobile app records location points and
uploads them in batches to your account before the track is finalized. Tracks
and their points are owner-scoped, so other users cannot read your position.

## Authentication

### How do I create an account?

On the web, select **Sign up** and enter an email address and password, or
continue with Google or Apple. Email sign-up sends you to the sign-in page
with a reminder to check your email and confirm the account.

### Why am I sent to Sign in?

The app pages require an authenticated session. If you open a protected page
without a session, DontDieFishing sends you to **Sign in** and retains the
requested path so you can continue there after authentication. If the session
has expired, sign in again and retry the original page.

### I cannot sign in

- Confirm the email address and password are entered, then submit the form
  again.
- If you just created the account, complete the confirmation link in your
  email before signing in.
- If you used Google or Apple to create the account, use that provider again.
- If an authentication error remains, try again after checking your network
  connection. The form displays the provider's error instead of silently
  treating the attempt as successful.

## Spots and conditions

### A spot near me is missing

The spot database is growing. Tap **Suggest a Spot** on the map (long-press
any location) to submit a new fishing spot. Include the name, water type, and
access notes.

### The conditions look wrong, stale, or incomplete

The app can show a dash when an individual value is unavailable, and the
track replay can label a missing risk score **UNKNOWN**. That is missing
source data, not a GO signal. Forecasts can also show **Forecast unavailable
right now**.

If data looks stale or a condition request fails:

1. Check the app's online state and reconnect if the offline banner says
   **You're offline — showing cached data. Scores may not be current.**
2. Reload the page or use the page's retry/refresh action.
3. Remember that gauges and forecast sources can be temporarily unavailable.
   Community reports supplement official data; they do not replace it.

On the web, the site list can also show **Create a vessel profile in Settings
to see conditions**. Create a vessel profile, then refresh the conditions.

### Can I use this for saltwater fishing?

Yes. Surf, pier, jetty, and inshore spots include tide data from NOAA CO-OPS
and saltwater species profiles.

## Network and permissions

### The app says it cannot connect

Mobile list screens show **Unable to connect to the network. Please check
your connection.** when a data load fails and provide **Retry**. Community
reports also provide retry actions for an initial load or a later refresh.
Reconnect, select **Retry**, and avoid treating cached or missing conditions as
current.

The web app shows an offline banner and may display cached data while the
browser is offline. Reconnect before relying on a score, then reload the page
to request current data.

### Why cannot I start GPS track recording?

Track recording requires foreground location permission. The mobile app asks
for background location permission as a second step; background permission
allows the slower background recording task, while foreground recording still
works without it. If foreground permission is denied, recording cannot start.
Grant location access in the device settings and try **Record** again.

### Why are alerts not arriving?

**Web push:** Allow browser notifications for DontDieFishing in the web app,
then:

- Check that the saved spot has alerts toggled on (spot page → bell icon).
- Alerts fire on status transitions (for example, green to yellow), not
  continuously.

**Native mobile push is not active.** Native delivery is pending DDF-39 and
device verification; changing phone notification permissions cannot make
native alerts arrive.

## Community reports

### How do I report current conditions or a hazard?

Use **Community Reports** on the mobile site detail screen. A report requires
one condition rating from **1 to 5**:

- **5 — Great**
- **4 — Good**
- **3 — Okay**
- **2 — Rough**
- **1 — Dangerous**

You can optionally add text for **Wind**, **Waves**, **Visibility**, and
**Notes**. Reports are recent community observations, not official forecasts.
The list shows reports from the **last 24 hours** for that site.

If loading or submitting a report fails, the screen shows an error and a
**Retry** action. A report is not a separate hazard-type form with a required
description or photo.
