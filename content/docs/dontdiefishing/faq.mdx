Common questions about the DontDieFishing web app and mobile functionality
present in source code.

## Plans and accounts

### Is DontDieFishing free?

The current source lists Free access to core launch-site, condition, and trip
screens. Pro adds a seven-day Fishable Days response, week-ahead comparison, and
a next-GO-day summary. The app currently lists $4.99/month or $39.99/year; the
current checkout display is authoritative and can change.

### How do I create an account?

Use **Sign up** with email and password or an available provider. Google is
available; Apple sign-in is shown only when its provider is configured. Email
confirmation may be required before the first sign-in. Protected app pages
require an authenticated session.

### Is there a public mobile app?

The repository's release status says no public App Store or Google Play build
has reached users. Source code for mobile screens is not proof of store
availability. Use the web app unless current official store listings confirm a
release.

## Launch sites, location, and privacy

### Do I need to share my location to browse sites?

No. Browse Launch Sites by region. The web map uses the selected region's map
view and its security policy disables browser geolocation. The optional mobile
site map also uses site/catalog data rather than phone GPS. Neither browsing
flow asks for your location, so denying browser or device location access does
not require a location-permission fallback to continue browsing.

### What happens if I deny location permission?

Mobile GPS track recording requires foreground location permission. Background
access is optional: if you deny it, foreground recording can continue, but the
background task cannot. If foreground access is denied, track recording cannot
start; grant it in device settings before trying again. See [GPS tracks](/dontdiefishing/tracks).

### Are saved or custom sites public?

Saving a curated site changes only your account's saved list. A custom launch
site that you place on the web map is owner-scoped, non-curated, and automatically
saved to the creating account; it is not a public suggestion or a shared map
submission. A float-plan link explicitly shares that trip's launch site and plan
details with anyone holding the link. Do not post the link publicly; revoke it
when no longer needed. See [Finding spots](/dontdiefishing/finding-spots) and
[Trip plans and safety](/dontdiefishing/trips-and-safety).

### Can other users read recorded GPS tracks?

Other users cannot read your recorded tracks or their GPS points; access is
scoped to the owning account. See [GPS tracks](/dontdiefishing/tracks).

### Does the app recommend tackle or fishing gear?

No fishing-tackle recommendation catalog is documented in the current app
routes. The web app has vessel profiles and personal vessel records such as
notes, maintenance, photos, and documents; those are not tackle advice.

## Scores, forecasts, and regulations

### Why is a score missing, stale, or incomplete?

A dash or **NO DATA** can mean that no timestamped trusted snapshot is available.
**STALE** means the last result is more than 30 minutes old. Some sources can be
unavailable even when the snapshot has a timestamp. Reconnect, refresh or retry,
check the age and available-signal count, and verify directly with NWS or NOAA.
Never read a missing value as safe.

### Are forecasts guarantees? Does GO mean I can launch?

No. Forecasts are probabilistic and future scores omit some live marine inputs,
including active warnings, waves, tides, currents, water temperature, and UV.
**GO** is a modeled result from available inputs, not a safety clearance. Check
current official warnings and conditions immediately before departure.

### Does a regulation status prove a closure or open season?

No. The catalog contains selected cited saltwater-rule references. **VERIFY**
means the app cannot establish current status; it is neither OPEN nor CLOSED.
Even **OPEN** or **CLOSED** can be superseded by an emergency order or local
restriction. Follow the linked authority before fishing or keeping a catch.

### What do tides show?

Tide height/phase is based on NOAA CO-OPS predictions where a station and site
timezone are configured; current predictions may be unavailable. It is not a
measured local water level. Future Fishable Days scores do not include tides or
tidal current.

## Reports and alerts

### Are Community Reports official hazard reports?

No. The site-detail form accepts a 1–5 condition rating with optional wind,
waves, visibility, or notes; it lists recent reports from the last 24 hours.
These are attributed user observations, not verified sensor data, official
forecasts, or an emergency-response channel.

### Why are alerts not arriving?

Web Push requires a signed-in browser with HTTPS notification permission and an
active subscription. Saved-site events are transition-based, not continuous.
Monitored trips are checked every 30 minutes and can create a separate status
event. Do not assume a push was seen or that its conditions remain current.
Native push code is not verified for a public store release; saved-site alerts
do not use email or SMS.

## Network and replay

### The app says it cannot connect

Reconnect and use the page's **Retry** or refresh action. The offline web app
may display cached content; verify that condition age is current before relying
on it. A failed data request is not a GO result.

### Why can't I export a GPS track?

Only a finalized track can be exported. Finish recording, wait for finalization,
then choose **EXPORT GPX** on the web replay. The file is capped at 20,000
points. Mobile recording depends on foreground location permission and the
mobile app's release status.
