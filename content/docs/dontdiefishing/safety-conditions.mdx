Data sources, hazard categories, and the limitations of DontDieFishing's
safety information.

## Data sources

DontDieFishing aggregates conditions from multiple authoritative sources:

| Data                | Source                                     | Refresh       |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------- |
| Stream level & flow | USGS NWIS gauges                           | 15 min        |
| Weather & forecasts | NOAA / NWS                                 | Hourly        |
| Tide data           | NOAA CO-OPS                                | 6 min         |
| Water temperature   | USGS + community                           | Hourly        |
| Flood warnings      | NWS AHPS                                   | Real-time     |
| Hazard reports      | Community + land managers                  | As reported   |
| Fishing regulations | State fish & wildlife agencies (23 states) | Daily recheck |

<Callout>
  Fishing regulations — size limits, bag limits, and seasonal closures — are
  sourced from official state fish and wildlife agencies for all 23
  launch-site states. Each regulation card on a spot page links directly to
  its official source and shows a last-checked date. A daily automated
  recheck fingerprints agency pages and flags rules for re-curation when
  content changes. Regulation data is a reference summary, not a complete
  rulebook; always consult the official agency page before fishing.
</Callout>

## Safety status

The green / yellow / red status is computed from:

- **Green** — no active warnings, water within normal range, weather favorable
- **Yellow** — elevated but not dangerous: rising water, moderate wind,
  reduced visibility, or a nearby (not spot-specific) advisory
- **Red** — active danger: flood stage exceeded, lightning within 10 miles,
  strong current advisory, or a spot-specific hazard report

Status updates automatically as conditions change. You can enable push alerts
for saved spots to be notified on status transitions.

## Hazard categories

- **Current** — strong or unexpected water flow (dam releases, tidal surge)
- **Flood** — water above safe wading/fishing levels
- **Weather** — lightning, high wind, extreme cold/heat
- **Access** — unsafe banks, washed-out roads, closed areas
- **Water quality** — harmful algae bloom, contamination advisory
- **Wildlife** — alligator, snake, or large predator activity

## Limitations

<Callout type="warn">
  DontDieFishing is a decision-support tool, not a guarantee of safety.
  Conditions can change faster than data refreshes. Community reports are
  unverified observations. Always assess conditions in person before entering
  the water, and never fish alone in remote areas.
</Callout>

- USGS gauges are point measurements — conditions may differ upstream or
  downstream of the gauge
- Weather forecasts are probabilistic, not deterministic
- Community reports reflect one observer's judgment at one point in time
- Some spots have no nearby gauge or weather station; these show limited data
- Regulation summaries are transcribed from official sources on the date
  shown; in-season emergency orders may not yet be reflected

## Reporting a hazard

Tap **Report Hazard** on any spot page. Select the hazard type, add a
description and optional photo, and submit. Reports are reviewed and typically
appear within one hour. Urgent hazards (immediate danger to life) are flagged
for priority review.
