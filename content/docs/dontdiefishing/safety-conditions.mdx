DontDieFishing combines available environmental inputs into a lower-is-better
planning score for the selected launch site and vessel profile. It is a decision
aid, not a safety clearance. The score cannot establish that a trip is safe,
that a launch is open, or that a species may legally be retained.

<Callout type="warn">
  Forecasts are probabilistic, observations can be delayed or incomplete, and
  conditions can change faster than a refresh. Verify current weather and marine
  warnings with the official source, check applicable closures and fishing rules,
  and assess the site before departure. Never treat GO, a blank field, or an
  unavailable source as permission to launch.
</Callout>

## Condition sources

The web aggregator requests available sources in parallel and timestamps the
resulting snapshot. It does not promise a fixed refresh cadence for every
provider or station.

| Data shown or used                                                          | Source                                                                                                  | Availability and limits                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wind, gusts, air temperature, visibility, precipitation, lightning estimate | National Weather Service (NWS) forecast                                                                 | Forecast values are location- and time-dependent; some values can be unavailable. Lightning and visibility may be inferred from forecast text.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Waves, period, swell direction, and fallback wind or water temperature      | NOAA National Data Buoy Center (NDBC), when a buoy is configured; NWS grid data may provide wave values | Buoy readings are observations at a station, not conditions everywhere along a route. NWS grid wave values are forecasts, not buoy observations; a missing buoy reading does not mean seas are calm. Missing values remain unavailable.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Active marine warnings                                                      | NWS alerts                                                                                              | The displayed alert is a snapshot; it can change after the update. Launch sites behind Coast Guard regulated bars also poll the NWS marine zone for their bar (for example, Columbia River Bar or Grays Harbor Bar), so bar-water products such as a Hazardous Seas Warning or Gale Warning are included. A failed alert check is shown as "NWS alert status unavailable" rather than a clear-sky result. |
| Tide height/phase                                                           | NOAA CO-OPS high/low predictions                                                                        | The snapshot uses the next predicted high or low tide when a station and site timezone are available. A prediction is not an observed water level.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Tidal-current speed                                                         | NOAA CO-OPS current predictions                                                                         | Only available for a configured station and bin; the closest prediction is used.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Water temperature                                                           | NOAA CO-OPS, with NDBC buoy fallback                                                                    | Not available at every site.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| UV index                                                                    | U.S. EPA UV data                                                                                        | May be unavailable.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Sunrise and sunset                                                          | Calculated from site coordinates and timezone                                                           | Context only; not a scored safety signal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Water quality                                                               | Not currently supplied by the conditions aggregator                                                     | It is unavailable, not a claim that water is clean.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

USGS streamflow and water-quality measurements are not current inputs to this
launch-site conditions aggregator. Do not interpret the score as a river-gauge
or water-quality assessment.

## Score and status

The shared scorer clamps its 0–100 result and maps it to these bands:

|  Score | Status      | Meaning                                                                        |
| -----: | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|   0–39 | **GO**      | Lower modeled risk from the available scored inputs.                           |
|  40–59 | **CAUTION** | Elevated modeled risk; inspect the contributing signals and official guidance. |
| 60–100 | **NO-GO**   | High modeled risk, or a hard veto has applied.                                 |

The selected vessel type supplies default environmental thresholds. You can
choose a **Cautious**, **Moderate**, or **Experienced** profile and configure
custom thresholds or weights. The scorer uses wind/gusts, wave height, marine
warnings, visibility, tidal current, lightning, precipitation, water quality,
UV, water temperature, and optional travel time when those signals are
available. Tide height/phase, sunrise/sunset, air temperature, wave period, and
swell direction can be displayed without being scored.

### Hard vetoes

The score returns 100 / **NO-GO** before building weighted signal rows when a
veto applies. Current vetoes include an active NWS marine alert matching the
marine alert vocabulary — such as Small Craft Advisory (including for Rough
Bar), Gale Warning, Storm Warning, Hurricane Force Wind Warning, Hazardous Seas
Warning, Storm Surge Warning, Extreme Wind Warning, Heavy Freezing Spray
Warning, Special Marine Warning, Tsunami Warning, or Typhoon Warning; high
lightning risk; wind, gusts, waves, or tidal current above 1.5 times the selected
vessel threshold; visibility below half its threshold; or water temperature
below 75% of its threshold. Marine alert matching is prefix-based against the
NWS alert vocabulary, so a land product that merely contains a marine phrase
(for example, a Winter Storm Warning) does not count as a marine warning. Other
warnings can still be shown without triggering a veto. Read the full
[scoring guide](/dontdiefishing/how-scoring-works) for the exact calculations.

A NOAA or NWS marine warning is not the same as a fishing-regulation closure.
Rule status is a separate cited reference and is not a substitute for checking
an official closure, emergency order, or access restriction.

## Freshness and confidence

The current web interface labels snapshot age as **FRESH**, **AGING**, **STALE**,
or **NO DATA**:

- **FRESH** — at most 15 minutes old.
- **AGING** — more than 15 and at most 30 minutes old.
- **STALE** — older than 30 minutes; it is a last-known result, not a current
  conditions check.
- **NO DATA** — missing, invalid, or future timestamp; no trusted score is
  shown.

The authenticated conditions API reuses a snapshot only while it is under 30
minutes old. Otherwise it fetches new conditions; if the aggregate fetch fails,
it returns an error instead of calculating a score from an old snapshot. The
site-detail page renders request-time data and labels a last-known stale result
as stale. A failed update may leave the last labeled result visible with an
error and retry action; do not mistake it for a successful refresh.

The **HIGH / MED / LOW** confidence label counts how many score signals are
available. It is a data-completeness description, not a statistical probability
that the score or forecast is correct. A timestamp also does not prove that
every provider succeeded. Some individual sources can fail independently and
leave fields unavailable or warnings missing.

Missing numeric signals do not contribute weight; the scorer redistributes the
remaining available weights. Some provider fallbacks can still understate an
unknown hazard. Treat a missing, stale, degraded, or incomplete input as
unknown, check official NWS/NOAA sources, and make the decision using current
conditions and your own judgment.

<Callout type="warn">
  A failed marine-alert check is no longer read as a clear sky: the page shows
  **NWS alert status unavailable** and the incomplete check contributes a mild
  advisory score instead of an available zero-risk marine-warning signal. It
  does not yet cap the verdict at CAUTION, however, and the forecast failure
  fallback can still supply `"none"` for lightning. If numeric conditions are
  missing and only that zero-valued default remains, the current scorer can
  return **0 / GO**. The aggregate can timestamp that result as fresh; the
  separate **LOW** completeness label does not withhold the favorable score.
  Neither a fresh timestamp nor an unavailable alert status proves that hazards
  were checked successfully. Verify official conditions and warnings
  independently.
</Callout>

## Forecasts and tides

The site-detail weather card shows up to seven NWS daily forecast rows when
available. A forecast is not a guarantee and is not the same as a current
condition snapshot. The Fishable Days forward score uses only forecast-derived
inputs and omits live marine warnings, waves, tides, tidal current, water
temperature, and UV. The score does not promise conditions at a future hour.

Tide height/phase comes from NOAA CO-OPS predictions where station metadata is
configured; current speed may also be unavailable. Predictions and solunar
windows are context, not a measured reading, safe-launch certification, or
fish-catch guarantee. See [Fishable Days](/dontdiefishing/fishable-days).
