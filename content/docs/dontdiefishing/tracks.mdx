A mobile GPS session can be linked to a planned trip or started as an ad-hoc
recording. Finalized sessions can be reviewed on the authenticated web app's
**Tracks** page.

## Start a recording and location permissions

Open **Trips** in the mobile app and select **Record** on a trip, or start an
ad-hoc session. Foreground location permission is required. Background
permission is requested separately and is optional: if it is declined,
foreground recording can still work, but the background task cannot continue
recording while the app is backgrounded.

The recorder uses phone GPS, samples about once per second in the foreground and
more slowly in the background when permission is granted, skips points that
have moved less than roughly five metres, and caps a session at 20,000 points.
Speed and course are included when supplied by the device. Stop the recording
when the session is complete; the app uploads points in batches and finalizes
the track.

## Offline recording and retry

Track points and session metadata are kept on the device while recording. If an
upload or finalization fails, points remain locally and the app reports that it
will retry. Pending uploads are flushed when Trips starts again. An interrupted
or dead prior session can be recovered without overwriting its unuploaded
points; stop a live session before starting another.

## Replay on the web

Sign in and open **Tracks**. The recent-session list shows up to 20 sessions,
newest first, with a linked launch site or **Ad-hoc session**. Open sessions are
marked **LIVE**. Replay includes the path map, play/pause, a progress control,
elapsed time, 1× / 2× / 4× speeds, current playhead speed, and a speed profile.

Metrics can include distance in nautical miles, duration, maximum and average
speed in knots, point count, and a current score/status when the linked trip's
launch-site conditions can be loaded. These conditions are current at replay
time, not proof of what the weather was during the recorded track. An ad-hoc
track without a linked launch site has no site-based live score.

When recorded AIS data is available, **AIS nearby** shows up to the three
closest contacts at the playhead, and **SHOW AIS TRACKS** displays recorded AIS
paths. AIS is absent when the session has no AIS records.

## Export GPX

A **finalized** track can be downloaded with **EXPORT GPX**. An open track must
be finalized first. The export is a GPX 1.1 track file and is capped at 20,000
points; a failed export reports an error so you can retry. The control is not
disabled as a placeholder.
