Infolitico publishes a small set of faith-framed articles each day. There is no
account to create and no paywall — open the site, read today's headlines, and
spend two minutes with the reflection.

## The daily rhythm

1. **Open [infolitico.com](https://infolitico.com)** — the day's stories are
   listed with their headlines.
2. **Read the news first.** Each article leads with one or two factual
   paragraphs of straight reporting on a real current event.
3. **Read the reflection.** The spiritual layer connects the event to a
   relevant Bible verse and closes with a short prayer.
4. **Share it.** The site is written for a reader who would pass a story to a
   friend in a church group chat or text it to a parent.

## What an article contains

| Section            | What it is                                                                                |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Headline**       | The event framed through a faith/values lens                                              |
| **Verse**          | A Bible verse from the curated database, reproduced exactly (when the story warrants one) |
| **The News**       | 1–2 factual paragraphs, straight reporting                                                |
| **The Reflection** | 2–3 paragraphs connecting the event to the verse's teaching                               |
| **Prayer**         | A short closing prayer tied to the reflection                                             |

Articles run 300–500 words — about a two-minute read.

## What Infolitico is not

- **Not political commentary.** The site never uses party names, candidate
  endorsements, or left/right framing. Its sensibility is values-based:
  family, personal responsibility, stewardship, gratitude, character,
  community, and faith in God's plan.
- **Not a theology journal or a prosperity-gospel platform.** The reflection
  is a warm, conversational devotional — not a sermon, a lecture, or a
  prophecy hotline.
- **Not satire or parody.** Every story reports a real event that actually
  happened.

## Newsletter

Prefer email? The morning newsletter delivers the day's stories to your
inbox. See the [newsletter guide](/newsletter).

<Callout type="warn">
  Infolitico is fully automated. The pipeline's rejection behavior is
  intentional: stories without a genuine faith connection are not published
  rather than forced into a devotional frame.
</Callout>
