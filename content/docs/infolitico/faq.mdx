## Is Infolitico written by humans?

No. Infolitico is fully autonomous: sourcing, drafting, guarding, and
publishing run without human editorial writers. The pipeline's safety gates
are enforced in code and database policy, not by prompts.

## How do I know the news is real?

Every article reports a real event from a curated public feed, and the
pipeline requires a nonempty factual source summary before any drafting
happens. Source provenance (discovery URL and verified publisher URL) is
carried through the pipeline. Stories that cannot be factually summarized are
rejected.

## How are Bible verses chosen?

Deterministically. A SHA-256 hash of the source event's ID selects the verse
index from the curated `bible_verses` database — the same event always yields
the same verse. Verses are reproduced exactly from that database; the
pipeline never fabricates or modifies Scripture text.

## Do all articles include a verse?

No. When the pipeline's verse selector determines a verse is not recommended
for a story, the article publishes with a reflection but no verse blockquote.

## What about the commentary quotes I see in articles?

Commentary quotes come from a curated `commentary_quotes` table, are attached
in code (never invented by a model), and appear at most one per article —
verbatim and attributed.

## Why do some stories get rejected?

Rejection is the pipeline's primary safety mechanism. A story with no genuine
faith connection (`no_natural_faith_connection`), insufficient factual
evidence, or a failed safety gate is not published — it is never forced into
a devotional frame.

## Is Infolitico political?

No. The site's sensibility is values-based — family, personal responsibility,
stewardship, gratitude, character, community, and faith in God's plan. It
never uses party names, candidate endorsements, or left/right framing.

## Is Infolitico satire?

No. Every story reports a real event that actually happened.

## How much does it cost?

The site is free to read. The morning newsletter is available by subscribing
on [infolitico.com](https://infolitico.com).
