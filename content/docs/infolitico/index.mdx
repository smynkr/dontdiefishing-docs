Infolitico is a fully autonomous, faith-framed news site. Every article reports
a real event from today's news cycle accurately, then connects it to a relevant
Bible verse with a short spiritual reflection — a morning devotional that
starts with today's headlines.

<Callout type="warn">
  Infolitico is fully automated end to end: sourcing, drafting, guarding, and
  publishing run without human editorial writers. The pipeline protects source
  provenance, exact curated Scripture text, and editorial safety before any
  story can publish.
</Callout>
